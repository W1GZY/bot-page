2. Privacy Policy

SeanBot is an application running on the Discord platform. Use of the Bot is also subject to Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which we are not responsible for. This policy outlines the types of information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Our role for server data

For data that belongs to a particular Discord server, SeanBot generally acts as a service provider for that server. The server owner and staff decide which features to enable, how to configure them, and where logs or transcripts are sent. SeanBot provides the tools and processes the data needed to run those features, but it does not control the server itself and does not decide how the server uses its own data.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or store your Discord password or authentication tokens. We do not keep your full message history across servers. We do not sell personal data. We do not use your content or activity data for advertising or unrelated promotional purposes. We do not build cross-server profiles about individual members from unrelated servers.

In short

  • This policy covers SeanBot the Discord bot and the SeanBot website.
  • We collect different things depending on which features a server turns on.
  • Server staff control which features are enabled in their own server.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect the data we hold against unauthorized access, loss, and misuse. We do not represent or warrant that our measures will prevent every breach, leak, or unauthorized access. If we become aware of a security incident that may affect personal data, we will take reasonable steps to address it and will notify affected users or server owners where appropriate and where required by law.

Legal requests and disclosures

We may disclose data where we are required to do so by law, where necessary to protect the safety of people or servers, or where another legally valid request applies. We do not publish or advertise involuntary disclosures, and any such disclosure is limited to what is necessary for the purpose at issue.

2.1 What we collect

We only collect data that a feature needs in order to work. Most of it comes from the Discord API, from the server's settings, or from information you or a server enter into a feature. We do not collect extra personal data from you or from server members just because it is available through the Discord API.

Identity data from Discord

When a feature needs it, we may use Discord identity information such as a username, numerical Discord ID, avatar hash, or public flags. We do not take more identity data than the feature needs.

Server and configuration data

Each server that adds SeanBot has its own settings. We store the server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and other configuration needed to run the features that server enabled. That can include things like log channel choices, ticket settings, report settings, timezone settings, and server-specific module settings.

What you tell the bot

Some features rely on information you submit directly. Examples include timezone choices, event sign-ups, ticket text, report text, appeal text, and Wizard101 profile details such as wizard names, schools, and levels. We store that information only for the server and feature it was submitted to.

Activity summaries and timestamps

Some features keep usage information for a server. This is usually metadata or summaries rather than the full text of everything said in the server. Examples include last-message timestamps, voice or message activity summaries, emoji usage summaries, server counters, and command or interaction records used by that server.

Short-lived deletion and edit snapshots

If a server enables message-deletion or edit logging, and where message content storage is allowed by the Bot's configuration, we may keep a short-lived snapshot of recently deleted messages so the log can reconstruct what was removed. Depending on that server's settings, a snapshot may include a truncated version of the message text, embed titles or descriptions, attachment links, and counts of mentions, role mentions, or @everyone/@here usage. We do not store your full message history across the server. These snapshots are not kept for every server or every feature, they expire automatically after a configured retention period, and they are removed once they have been used for a deletion log.

Website and operational data

The SeanBot website and the Bot itself may generate technical and operational data needed to run them, such as connection, usage, or error data. If any such data is collected, it is limited to what is needed for operation, debugging, security, or service improvement, and is handled under the same approach described in this policy. We do not use this data to build profiles about you beyond what the related feature requires. Cookies and other browser storage are covered separately in the Cookie Policy.

Third-party services

The Bot and the SeanBot website may rely on third-party services such as Discord, hosting providers, database providers, analytics or error-reporting services, and security providers. Those third parties collect and handle their own data under their own terms and privacy practices, which this policy does not cover. Where a third-party service collects usage data, it does so under its own policy, not under this one. If you use the Bot, you are also subject to the terms and privacy practices of any third-party services it depends on.

Quick note on retention. Different kinds of data are kept for different lengths of time. Server configuration and feature data is generally kept while the bot remains in that server and may persist briefly after removal so things like moderation or ticket history stay usable to the server. Deletion/edit snapshots are short-lived and expire automatically. User-specific data is generally deleted or de-linked when the bot leaves the user’s last shared server or on a valid deletion request, subject to the limits in this policy. Anonymized and aggregated usage data may be kept longer. The full retention details are in section 2.2.

2.5 What Server Staff Can See

SeanBot is configured, controlled, and observed by the administrators and staff of each individual Discord server that adds it. Those server staff members are not SeanBot owners or developers; they are the community running the server. When you join or interact with a server using SeanBot, that server's staff may be able to see some of your activity through the bot. What they can see depends on which features that server enabled, and where they chose to send logs.

Data visible to server staff through SeanBot

  • Moderation actions and warnings. If a server uses moderation, warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, or related logging, staff can view warning history and moderation points for members of that server, along with the reason, timestamp, and staff member who issued each action. Those records are stored for that server, not across all servers you join.
  • Event, sign-up, and roster data. If you sign up for raids, events, polls, team-ups, or register a Wizard101 wizard, that server's staff can see the information you submitted through those features inside their server (for example your chosen name, school, level, or event sign-up).
  • Reports and ticket content. If you file a report, appeal, or support ticket in a server, the staff members with access to that review process may be able to read what you wrote, including any message content, attachments, or reason you provided. Ticket transcripts may be saved as HTML archives and shared with assigned staff or the ticket creator, depending on that server's settings.
  • Server activity, voice, and analytics. If a server enables activity, voice, emoji, or server statistics features, staff may see aggregated or per-member usage information for that server, such as message or voice participation, emoji usage trends, server counters, or last-message timestamps. These are typically summaries or timestamps rather than the full text of what you said.
  • Deleted and edited message logs. If a server turns on message-deletion or edit logging, SeanBot may keep a short-lived snapshot of recently deleted messages so it can reconstruct what was removed. Depending on that server's settings and the bot's configuration, those snapshots may include a truncated version of the message text, embed titles or descriptions, attachment links, and counts of mentions, role mentions, or @everyone/@here usage. They do not include your full message history across the server, they are not stored for every server or every feature, and they expire automatically after a configured retention period. They are also removed once they have been used for a deletion log.
  • Timezones and personal settings. If you set a timezone or similar personal preference in a server, staff with access to that server's configuration may see that setting as it exists in that server.

What server staff generally cannot see through SeanBot

  • Your activity in other servers. A server's staff can only see data that SeanBot recorded inside their own server. They do not get a view of your activity in other servers that also use SeanBot.
  • Your private DMs with the bot for public server features. Where a feature is meant to be public to a server, private bot DMs are not a hidden channel for staff to read. Server-specific data is tied to that server's configuration.
  • Your account credentials or Discord login. SeanBot never receives or stores your Discord password or authentication tokens. Server staff do not get those either.
  • More than the server enabled. If a server does not enable a feature, the related data is generally not collected for that server. Staff visibility is limited to the features and logging channels that server actually configured.

Important: Server owners and staff are responsible for the server they run. SeanBot provides the tools and logs; we do not control how a server's staff use, store, export, or share the data their own server generates. If a server enables transcript archives, moderation logs, or report reviews, those materials may be readable by the staff members with access to them, and may be exported or retained outside of SeanBot at that server's discretion. We are not responsible for misuse by server staff. If you are concerned about a specific server, the most direct remedy is to leave that server or ask its staff what they log and how long they keep it.

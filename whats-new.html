What's new

The latest changes to SeanBot, newest first. Each entry links to the guide or page that covers the work.

11 November 2026 Emoji Manager

Never Used, and a library that stays in order

The Emoji Manager now lists custom emojis with no recorded uses and lets an administrator select and delete them in one pass. An emoji younger than seven days, locked to a role, named by an alias, managed by an app, or missing an upload date is marked and left out of the selection. The bot rechecks those rules against the live server before deleting anything.

The library also keeps its own order now: drag a tile to set the order used here and in the emoji picker, or sort by uses or name for a moment. Usage counts follow the emoji id, so a rename no longer resets its history.

Read the Emoji Manager guide

11 November 2026 Presence

Presence, from the live board to the weeks behind it

Presence is now four tabs: Right now, Insights, Week history and Watch list. The live board keeps the per-member clock and the recent changes. Insights adds hours online in your timezone, the shape of the week, the members who spend the most time online, what the server plays and the split between desktop, mobile and web. Week history keeps the weeks after they end.

A raid-time hint reads the last four weeks and waits for four hours of tracked time before it names a day, so one quiet evening cannot look like a pattern.

Read the Presence guide

9 November 2026 Logging

Watch one member's presence

Presence watching follows one member instead of the whole server. A state change posts the old and new state, the activity and a timestamp. The list is saved and survives a restart, which reports the move it slept through instead of adopting the new state silently.

New members can be followed automatically for one to thirty days, with the earliest deadline giving way first. Naming an automatic entry with /log watch_presence promotes it to a watch that lasts until you remove it.

9 November 2026 Custom Bot

A name and picture for this server

Custom Bot Manager gives SeanBot its own nickname and server avatar without changing the bot anywhere else. Save a square image up to 512 KB, reset both with one button, and leave the rotating activity to the bot.

Read the Custom Bot Manager guide

6 November 2026 Server safety

Config Health, Config History, Bulk Role Changes and Permission Audit

Four administrator pages arrived together. Config Health finds channels and roles that no longer resolve, and repairs a whole area with an undo record. Config History keeps up to 20 copies of the settings document and shows a restore one setting at a time. Bulk Role Changes previews a role add or remove for a filtered set, then changes at most 500 members in one run. Permission Audit reports who holds Administrator, what @everyone grants and which roles sit out of reach, with fixes for an Administrator role and a dangerous @everyone grant.

Read the server safety guide

5 November 2026 Moderation

Vote Mutes

The server can vote to time a member out. A trigger emoji or /votemute start opens a card; members sign with the vote emoji, and the threshold is the larger of a floor and a share of eligible members present. Repeat votes climb a mute ladder, and voter, starter and immune roles decide who may sign, who may open a vote and who can never be the target. The dashboard and /votemute settings read the same record, and the audit can be exported as a CSV file.

Read the Vote Mutes guide

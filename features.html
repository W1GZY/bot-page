Every module, one place

SeanBot is a set of independent modules rather than one fixed feature list. Turn on the ones your server needs and leave the rest off; each has a page of its own in the documentation.

Moderation and anti-raid

AutoMod filters, raid detection, quarantine, timeouts, bans and a full audit log. Stops a flood before it becomes a problem.

Support tickets

Private threads with a panel, transcripts and staff roles. Members open a ticket instead of pinging a moderator.

Timezone roles

Give every member a role that matches where they are, then read the times in their own zone instead of guessing.

Cross-server federation

Link two or more servers so a message posted in one appears in the others, with per-link rules.

Polls and raid sign-ups

Quick polls, timed events and raid sign-up boards with capped slots and role-based teams.

Analytics and logging

Server statistics, emoji usage, voice activity and a configurable audit log, all searchable from the dashboard.

Event manager

Create events with sign-ups, reminders and a countdown, and post them as embeds your members can react to.

Embed builder

Design embeds with live preview, then send them to any channel or reuse them as welcome and farewell messages.

Emoji and sticker tools

Track which emoji your server actually uses, and manage custom emoji from the dashboard.

Giveaways

Run a giveaway with role requirements, entry limits and a fair draw, then announce the winner.

Welcome and farewell

Greet new members and mark departures with embeds that can carry a role or a DM.

Wizard101 tools

Roster, badge scanning, guild activity and raid boards for Wizard101 communities.
